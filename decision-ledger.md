# Decision ledger

Used in lesson 3. One row for every change or assumption an AI assistant made to your data.

**How to fill it in**

1. In the same chat as your analysis, ask the assistant to list every change or assumption as a table: what it did, how many rows it touched, how much it moved the total, what it assumed, and the one question to ask whoever produced the file. Ask for the biggest effect first.
2. Copy its table into the first four columns below.
3. Test what you can against the file itself, and write **supports**, **does not support** or **cannot tell** in "My check".
4. Ask the person who made the file. Mark each row **Confirmed**, **Open** or **Contradicted**.

## Status

- **Confirmed** — the person who made the file said the assumption is right.
- **Open** — nobody has answered, or the answer was "I don't know". Still a guess.
- **Contradicted** — the answer shows the assumption was wrong. Undo the decision and redo the number.
